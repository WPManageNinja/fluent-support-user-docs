# Personal Reports

The ‘**Personal Reports**’ section displays the ticket statistics and performance data specific to **you** as the logged-in support agent. This report is private to you and helps you track your own productivity.

Unlike the other pages in the Reports section, Personal Reports needs no special permissions — every agent can see their own numbers.

## Accessing Personal Reports

Go to the **Reports** tab in the dashboard menu and select **Personal Reports** from the report sidebar.

Use the **Date Range** picker at the top of the page to scope your statistics to a specific period.

![Personal Reports dashboard showing quick stats, My Statistics and the My Performance panel](/images/reports-analytics/reports/personal-report/personal-reports-overview.webp)

## Quick Stats

Five cards summarise your workload for the selected range:

* **Total Replies:** Replies you sent.
* **New Tickets:** Tickets that came to you in the range.
* **Closed Tickets:** Tickets you resolved.
* **Responses:** Your total response count.
* **Open Tickets:** Tickets currently assigned to you that are still open.

## My Statistics

A chart visualising your activity over time. Use the tabs in the top right of the card to switch between:

* **Resolve Stats** — how many tickets you closed.
* **Response Stats** — how many replies you sent.

## My Performance

The panel on the right tracks your efficiency metrics:

* **Interactions:** Distinct tickets you touched — one ticket counts once, however many replies you sent to it.
* **Waiting Tickets:** How many of your tickets are currently waiting on your reply.
* **Average Waiting:** How long the average waiting customer has been waiting on you.
* **Max Waiting:** The longest any single customer has been waiting on you.

> [!NOTE]
> The **My Performance** panel reflects your queue as it stands right now. The quick stats and the chart above it are scoped to your selected date range.

> [!TIP]
> Personal Reports has no Live / Snapshot selector — it is always live. If you need to compare your numbers against an earlier point in time, set the date range to the earlier period instead.
