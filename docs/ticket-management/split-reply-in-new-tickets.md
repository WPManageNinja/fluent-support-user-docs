# Split Reply in New Tickets

Fluent Support allows you to **Split** any reply into a new ticket i.e. if a customer raises a new issue in an ongoing ticket, you can separate the conversation and create a new ticket, and the separate tickets will appear on both the customer & support ends. This article will guide you through the whole process of doing it.

## Split Reply In New Tickets

First, go to your **Fluent Support** and click on **Tickets**.

Now go to your desired ticket section from **Assigned to Me**, **Unassigned**, **Bookmarks**, or a status view such as **Open** or **All**.

Open the specific ticket you want to split and hit the **Three dot button** on the right side of the customer reply you want to move into a new ticket.  

Now, click on the **Split Ticket** and a pop-up will appear.

![The three-dot menu of a customer reply with the Split Ticket option highlighted](/images/ticket-management/productivity-tools/split-reply-in-new-tickets/split-ticket-1.webp)

The popup will display all the options for splitting tickets e.g., **Ticket** **Title** , **Ticket** **Content** , **Ticket** **Product** , etc. And, once you are done, don’t forget to click the **Split Ticket** button.

![The Split Ticket popup with the ticket title, content, priority, product and mailbox fields](/images/ticket-management/productivity-tools/split-reply-in-new-tickets/split-ticket-2.webp)

You can see the **split tickets appeared separately** on both the customer & support ends.

![The original ticket showing a message that the conversation has been split as a new ticket](/images/ticket-management/productivity-tools/split-reply-in-new-tickets/split-ticket-3.webp)
