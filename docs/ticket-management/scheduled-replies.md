# Scheduled Replies

**Scheduled Replies** let you write a reply now and send it later. Pick the date and time, and Fluent Support sends it for you, so your customer gets your answer at the right moment.

::: info
Scheduled Replies is available with **Fluent Support Pro**.
:::

## Schedule a Reply

1. Open a ticket and write your reply in the editor.
2. Click the **More send options** arrow next to the send button.
3. Choose **Schedule reply…**.
4. Pick a **Date** and **Time**.
5. Turn on **Close ticket after it sends** if you want the ticket closed once the reply goes out.
6. Click **Schedule for later**.

![Schedule reply option in the send menu of a ticket](/images/ticket-management/daily-operations/scheduled-replies/schedule-menu.webp)

::: warning
Times use **UTC**, a world standard time that can differ from your own. Your local time is shown under the fields, so you can double-check before you schedule.
:::

Choose a time at least one minute from now, and make sure your reply is not empty.

## Manage a Scheduled Reply

A scheduled reply appears as a card on the ticket, showing when it sends. The ticket list also shows a **Reply scheduled** badge.

![Scheduled reply card on a ticket with Edit, Send now and Cancel reply actions](/images/ticket-management/daily-operations/scheduled-replies/scheduled-reply-card.webp)

From the card you can:

* **Edit** the reply text, attachments or send time.
* **Send now** if you no longer want to wait.
* **Cancel reply** to stop it. The text goes back to the editor as a draft. A confirmation asks first, and **Keep scheduled** leaves it as it is.

A ticket can have **one** scheduled reply at a time. To schedule another, edit or cancel the existing one first.

## When a Reply Is Paused

Fluent Support pauses a scheduled reply when something changes on the ticket, so an outdated reply does not go out by accident:

* The customer replies after you scheduled it.
* The ticket is closed after you scheduled it.

A paused reply shows a **Paused** label. Check the conversation, then click **Send as planned** to let it go out on schedule. You can also **Edit** it, use **Send now**, or **Cancel reply**.

A reply is also not sent if it could not go out on time, or if the agent who wrote it is no longer available. The card tells you which, so you can review it.
