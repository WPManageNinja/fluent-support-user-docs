# Bookmark Tickets

Fluent Support allows the support agents to Bookmark their tickets to find them easily on the dashboard or to request another agent to look at a ticket. This article will guide you through the whole process of doing it.

## Bookmarks

First, go to your **Fluent Support** and click on **Tickets**. Now go to your desired ticket section from **Assigned to Me**, **Unassigned**, **Bookmarks**, or a status view such as **Open** or **All**

Open the specific ticket you want to bookmark and click on the **Bookmark Icon** button.  

![The bookmark button in the ticket top bar highlighted](/images/ticket-management/productivity-tools/bookmark-tickets/ticket-bookmark-1.webp)

Now, an **Add Bookmark** popup will appear.

You can assign single or multiple support staff to the ticket using the dropdown list. After selecting the support staff, click the **Add** button and the ticket will be bookmarked & stored under the **Bookmarks** section on the left sidebar.

![The Add Bookmark popup with an agent selected and the Add button highlighted](/images/ticket-management/productivity-tools/bookmark-tickets/ticket-bookmark-2.webp)

The **support staff** (agents) will also be **bookmarked** on the right side of that ticket and will shown under the watcher section.

![The Watchers widget in the ticket sidebar listing the agent who bookmarked the ticket](/images/ticket-management/productivity-tools/bookmark-tickets/ticket-bookmark-3.webp)

You can add more support staff (agents) or remove by clicking on the **+ Add Watcher** button. 

![The Add Watcher option in the Watchers widget with the agent dropdown open](/images/ticket-management/productivity-tools/bookmark-tickets/ticket-bookmark-4.webp)
