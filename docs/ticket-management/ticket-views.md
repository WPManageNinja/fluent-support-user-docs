# Ticket Views

Ticket Views give every agent a clear starting point. The sidebar on the **Tickets** screen groups your queue into the places you check every day, and each view shows a live count. Click a view to open its tickets.

## Where To Find Ticket Views

Go to **FluentSupport > Tickets**. The views sit in the **Ticket Filters** sidebar on the left.

![The Ticket Filters sidebar with My Work, Needs Attention, Priority, Status and Tags views](/images/ticket-management/daily-operations/ticket-views/ticket-views-sidebar.webp)

## The Views

**My Work**
- **Assigned to Me:** open tickets assigned to you.
- **Unassigned:** open tickets that no agent owns yet. Agents who can only see their assigned tickets don't see this view.
- **Bookmarks** (Pro): tickets you [bookmarked](/bookmarks).

**Needs Attention**
- **Waiting For Reply:** tickets where the customer is waiting for an answer.

**Priority**
- One view per priority, with the highest priority on top. Critical tickets stand out.

**Status**
- One view per ticket status, such as **Open**, **Active**, **Closed**, **New** and **All**.

**Saved Filters** (Pro)
- Your [saved searches](/advanced-filter-save-search-feature). This group appears once you save a search.

**Tags**
- One view per [ticket tag](/ticket-tags-custom-tags). The sidebar shows your first eight tags. When you have more, a search box helps you find the rest.

## Live Counts

Each view shows how many tickets it holds, and the counts update as your team works. Counts follow each agent's access: an agent who only sees assigned tickets never sees numbers for tickets they can't open.

## Switch Views From a Single Ticket

You don't need to go back to the list to change queues.

- The **view switcher** at the top left of the single ticket screen shows the current view and its count. Click it to pick another view.
- Next to the ticket number, FluentSupport shows where you are in the list, such as **2 of 11**.
- Use the up and down arrows to open the previous or next ticket. They keep going across list pages, so you can work through a long queue without leaving the ticket screen.

![The view switcher and the list position on the single ticket screen](/images/ticket-management/daily-operations/ticket-views/ticket-views-single-ticket.webp)

## Ticket Views on Phones and Tablets

On a phone or tablet, the sidebar moves out of the way so the ticket list starts at the top of the screen. Tap the view name above the list to open **Ticket Views**. It opens as a bottom sheet on phones and as a side panel on tablets.

![Ticket Views as a bottom sheet on a phone](/images/ticket-management/daily-operations/ticket-views/ticket-views-phone.webp)

## Tips And Notes

::: tip Keyboard shortcuts
When [keyboard shortcuts](/navigate-with-keyboard-shortcut) are on, open a view from anywhere on the Tickets screen. Use **Ctrl + Shift** on Windows and Linux, or **Cmd + Shift** on a Mac:

- **A**: Open tickets
- **M**: Assigned to Me
- **U**: Unassigned
- **B**: Bookmarks (Pro)
:::

::: info
Click the collapse icon at the top of the sidebar for more room. The collapsed sidebar keeps the view icons.
:::
